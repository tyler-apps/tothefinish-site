What do my AI coaches watch when a runner starts at 258 lb?

I started this at 258 lb, chasing a sub-3 marathon at Chicago in October 2027. My coaches don't treat my size as a reason to shrink the goal. They treat it as a reason to watch a few things closely: how hard my easy runs really are, how my feet feel at first light, and whether I'm eating enough to do the work.

This is one heavier runner's setup, not medical advice.

What my coaches watch

WhatHowWhy
Easy runs stay easyA heart-rate range learned from my own easy runs (109 to 120 bpm right now)Pace alone would push me too hard on easy days
FeetA 0 to 10 check each morning, first steps out of bedPain I report goes to the top of the board and can change that day's run
Weight comes down slowlyNo more than about 2 lb a week, eating to hungerThe training needs the fuel
Stroller runsLogged for miles, left out of my pace readingsPushing a jogging stroller up a hill makes pace meaningless

What hasn't changed because of my weight

The goal. Speed comes first, a 5K in February and a 10K in May, then marathon training with hills every week and about 90 miles a week at the peak in August 2027. The numbers on this site will show whether a runner my size can carry that.
