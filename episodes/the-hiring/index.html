In October, the first time I asked my Garmin about a marathon, it said 4:39:49. My goal for Chicago is 2:59:59. That was the gap on the coaches’ first morning.

Kai wants the running done his way. Bo wants heavy legs, and Kai has something to say about when. Before I’ve run a mile for them, they have to agree on a week.

The figures in this episode are from the start of the story. The watch estimate changes with the training; the current numbers are on the home page.

All five coaches are animated AI characters with AI voices. The training is mine.
