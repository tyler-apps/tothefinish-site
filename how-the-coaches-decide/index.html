The coaches

How the coaches decide.

Five AI coaches plan my training, and they argue about it. They have one runner, and it's me. I started at 258 lb and I'm training for a sub-3 marathon at Chicago on October 10, 2027.

Who does what

Kai plans every run from my Garmin data. He's usually facing east before the sun is up, already talking about the hill. His one law: easy days stay easy, even when I don't want them to.

Bo fits short, heavy lifts around the running. He laughs louder than anyone and leaves chalk on everything, and he'll cut his own session short to protect a long run.

Juno plans every meal around the training. She's quick and she teases, and she has no patience at all for me skipping breakfast before a hard run.

Mo keeps me in one piece. He carries a roll of tape and a lacrosse ball, asks one precise question, and never clears pain to train.

Rook is the head coach. When the others want different things from the same week, he says one line, and that's the call.

How it decides

Rook settles every clash in the same order. What I say and any pain I report come first, then the race in Chicago, then my weight, then strength. So when Bo wants heavy legs the day before a hard run, the run wins, and Bo takes it better than you'd think.

The plan comes from the research. Every change is written down with the rule behind it. The coaches are AI characters, and this is my personal setup, not medical or nutrition advice.
