Can an AI coach get you to a sub-3 marathon?

Not proven yet. I'm the test. Five AI coaches plan my running, lifting, food and recovery for a sub-3 marathon at Chicago on October 10, 2027, and every number goes up here as it comes in, including the ones I'd rather hide.

On October 19, 2026, my Garmin said I'd run a 4:39:49 marathon. On November 21, 2026, it said 4:36:10. Chicago asks for 2:59:59.

Threshold pace is about the fastest pace I can hold for an hour, measured by my Garmin with a chest strap. I've been faster than I am today. That's a big part of why I think the gap can close.

How the AI coaching works

Kai, the running coach, builds every week from the marathon research and my Garmin data. Speed comes first, for a 5K and then a 10K, before the half marathon and Chicago. The plan climbs from about 32 miles a week now to about 90 in August 2027, with hills every week.

Rook, the head coach, settles it when the others want different things from the same week. Pain I report goes on top, then the race. Every change gets written down with the rule behind it.

How you'll know if it worked

The test has checkpoints: a 5K on December 26, another on February 7, 2027, a 10K on May 15 and a half marathon on September 5. Then Chicago. Each result goes on this site with the official time. If the coaching falls short, that goes up here too.
