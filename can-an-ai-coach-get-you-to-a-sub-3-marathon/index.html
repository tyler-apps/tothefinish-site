Can an AI coach get you to a sub-3 marathon?

Not proven yet. I'm testing it on myself. Five AI coaches plan my running, lifting, food and recovery for a sub-3 marathon at the Chicago Marathon on October 10, 2027, and I publish the numbers as they come in.

On October 19, 2026, my Garmin predicted a 4:39:49 marathon. On November 18, 2026, it said 4:34:04. I need 2:59:59.

How the AI coaching works

Kai, the running coach, builds every week from a published marathon training method and my Garmin data. The plan climbs from about 32 miles a week now to a peak near 90 in September 2027, with hill sprints every week.

Rook, the head coach, settles it when the running, strength and nutrition coaches want different things. Anything I report, like pain, comes first. Then the race. Every change is written down with the rule behind it.

How you'll know if it worked

The test has checkpoints: a 5K on February 6, 2027, a 10K on May 15, a half marathon on September 5, then Chicago. Each result goes on this site with the official time. If the AI coaching falls short, that goes on here too.
