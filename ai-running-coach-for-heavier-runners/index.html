Is there an AI running coach for heavier runners?

I started this at 258 lb, chasing a sub-3 marathon at Chicago in October 2027. My AI coaches don't treat that as a reason to go easy on the goal. They watch three things closely: how hard my easy runs really are, how my feet feel each morning, and whether I'm eating enough to run.

This is one heavier runner's setup, not medical advice.

What my coaches watch

WhatHowWhy
Easy runs stay easyA heart-rate range learned from my own easy runs (109 to 120 bpm right now)Pace alone would push me too hard on easy days
FeetA 0 to 10 check each morning, first steps out of bedPain I report is the one signal that changes a run that day
Weight comes down slowlyNo more than about 2 lb a week, eating to hungerThe training needs the fuel
Stroller runsLogged for miles, left out of my pace readingsPushing my son in the jogging stroller makes pace meaningless

What hasn't changed because of my weight

The goal. The plan still builds to sub-3 marathon training: hill sprints every week, fast pickups, long runs, and a peak near 90 miles a week in September 2027. The numbers on this site show whether that works.
