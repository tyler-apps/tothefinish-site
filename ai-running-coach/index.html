The coaches

My AI running coach.

My AI running coach is a team of five, and they argue. Right now they coach one runner: me. I started at 258 lb and I'm training for a sub-3 marathon at Chicago on October 10, 2027.

Who does what

Kai plans every run from my Garmin data. He's usually facing east before the sun is up, already talking about the hill. His one law: easy days stay easy, even when I don't want them to.

Bo fits short, heavy lifts around the running. He laughs louder than anyone and leaves chalk on everything, and he'll cut his own session short to protect a long run.

Juno plans every meal around the training. She's quick and she teases, and she has no patience at all for me skipping breakfast before a hard run.

Mo keeps me in one piece. He carries a roll of tape and a lacrosse ball, asks one precise question, and never clears pain to train.

Rook is the head coach. When the others want different things from the same week, he moves a card on the board, says one line, and that's the call.

How it decides

Rook settles every clash in the same order. Pain I report comes first, then the race in Chicago, then my weight, then strength. So when Bo wants heavy legs the day before a hard run, the run wins, and Bo takes it better than you'd think.

The plan comes from the research and from coaching methods that have built real marathoners. Every change is written down with the rule behind it. The coaches are AI characters, and this is my personal setup, not medical or nutrition advice.
