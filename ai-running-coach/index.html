The coaches

My AI running coach.

My AI running coach is a team of five, and they argue. Right now the setup coaches one runner: me, starting at 258 lb, training for a sub-3 marathon at Chicago on October 10, 2027.

Who does what

Kai plans every run from my Garmin data: the easy days, the hill sprints, the long run, and the paces each one should feel like.

Bo fits four short lifts around the running, and he always wants my legs on the wrong day.

Juno plans my meals around the training and makes sure I eat enough to run.

Mo keeps me healthy enough to keep training: sleep, aches and pains, prehab, and when I need an easier week.

Rook is the head coach. When the others want different things from the same week, he decides.

How it decides

Rook settles every clash in the same order: anything I report, like pain, comes first. Then the race in Chicago. Then my weight. Then strength. So when Bo wants heavy legs the day before a hard run, the run usually wins.

The plan comes from real coaching methods, and every change is written down with the rule behind it. The coaches are AI characters, and this is my personal setup, not medical or nutrition advice.
