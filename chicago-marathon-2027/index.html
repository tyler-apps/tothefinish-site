Quick answers

When is the 2027 Chicago Marathon?

Sunday, October 10, 2027. It starts and finishes in Grant Park.

When are the 2027 Chicago Marathon lottery results?

Drawing results go out by email on Tuesday, December 8, 2026. Runners who get in pay by Tuesday, December 15.

What is the Chicago Marathon qualifying time?

For 2027 it goes by your age on race day: 2:50 for men 16 to 34, 3:00 for men 40 to 44, 3:20 for women 16 to 34, 3:30 for women 40 to 44. The full table is above.

Can I still get into the 2027 Chicago Marathon?

The drawing and time-qualifier windows are closed. Charity entries are open until September 10, 2027, and tour operators start December 8, 2026.

How much does the Chicago Marathon cost?

$250 for US residents and $260 for everyone else, plus a processing fee. Charity entries are $325 and $335.

Is the Chicago Marathon course flat?

The race calls it flat and fast. It runs through 29 neighborhoods, and the time limit is 6 hours 30 minutes, a 15-minute mile.
