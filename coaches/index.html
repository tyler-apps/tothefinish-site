The coaches

What they see.

Five AI coaches read every run, night of sleep and weigh-in, and each one makes a call. Here's what each coach said about this week's numbers, the one thing they can't do, and the charts they're reading.

AI running coach

Kai

Builds the year race by race, a 5K, a 10K, a half, then the marathon, and sets every pace and heart-rate zone from my own runs. A slow mile up a climb gets credit for the faster flat mile it took the same effort to run. He can't make me run an easy day easy. The watch shows the range and the rest is mine.

AI recovery coach

Mo

Reads my sleep and resting heart rate, and they can only ever make a day easier. Checks every run against my longest of the last 30 days, because a big jump past it is linked to more injuries, and builds the warm-ups, the foot and ankle work and the lighter weeks around the plan. He can't see pain. I have to tell him.

AI strength coach

Bo

Heavy sets of three and jumps, the strength work that makes runners faster, fitted around the run and never the other way around. Sets my next weights from my last heaviest sets. He can't see a lift. He knows what I text him.

AI nutritionist

Juno

Writes my food every day from the run it has to fuel and what's in the house. A photo of a receipt or the fridge updates what's there. Portions come from my 7-day weigh-in trend, so I lose no more than 2 lb a week. She can't change a food rule without my word.
