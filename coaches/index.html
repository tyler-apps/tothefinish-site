AI running coach

Kai

Builds the year race by race, a 5K, a 10K, a half, then the marathon, and sets every pace and heart-rate zone from my own runs. A slow mile up a climb gets credit for the faster flat mile it took the same effort to run. He can't make me run an easy day easy. The watch shows the range and the rest is mine.

AI recovery coach

Mo

Reads my sleep and resting heart rate, and they can only ever make a day easier. Checks every run against my longest of the last 30 days, because a big jump past it is linked to more injuries, and builds the warm-ups, the foot and ankle work and the lighter weeks around the plan. He can't see pain. I have to tell him.

AI nutritionist

Juno

Writes my food every day from the run it has to fuel and what's in the house. A photo of a receipt or the fridge updates what's there. She checks my 7-day weigh-in trend against the work ahead. As the running grows, she feeds more of it. She can't change a food rule without my word.
