Is there an AI coach for running and lifting together?

Yes. Mine is a team: Kai plans the runs, Bo plans four short lifts a week, and Rook, the head coach, decides when they clash. The main rule: heavy leg work goes after the hard runs, never the day before one.

I'm using it to train for a sub-3 marathon at Chicago on October 10, 2027, and this is how my week actually looks.

My week, running and lifting together

DayRunLift
MondayEasy runSpeed bench press
TuesdayEasy run plus hill sprintsHeavy lower body, after the run
WednesdayEasy runNo lift
ThursdayRest or cross-trainHeavy upper body
FridayFartlek (fast pickups)Speed lower body, after the run
SaturdayRestNo legs: the long run is next
SundayLong runNo lift

Each lift is short, about 40 to 45 minutes, and always after that day's run.

How the coaches settle a clash

Bo, the strength coach, wants my legs strong. Kai, the running coach, wants them fresh for the hard days. When they disagree, Rook decides in the same order every time: anything I report, like pain, first. Then the marathon. Then my weight. Then strength. So the run usually wins.

Lower-body days sit on the same days as the hard runs, after the run, so the legs get a full day or more before the next hard effort. Saturday stays free of leg work before the Sunday long run.

Who this is for

Right now it coaches one runner: me. If it gets me to Chicago, the people on the waitlist get to try it first. It isn't medical advice, and the coaches are AI characters.
