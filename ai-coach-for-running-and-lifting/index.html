Is there an AI coach for running and lifting together?

Mine is a team. Kai plans the runs, Bo plans three short lifts a week, and Rook, the head coach, decides when they collide. The rule that settles most fights: heavy leg work goes on the hard-run days, hours after the run, never the day before one.

I'm using it to train for a sub-3 marathon at Chicago on October 10, 2027. This is what my week actually looks like right now.

My week, running and lifting together

DayRun (morning)Lift (about 1:30 PM)
MondayEasy run plus hill sprintsNo lift
TuesdayHill repeats or speed workHeavy lower body, sets of three
WednesdayEasy run on Mission TrailsNo lift
ThursdayRest or cross-trainUpper body, kept light enough to hold
FridayThreshold runJumps and power
SaturdayEasy runNo legs: the long run is next
SundayLong runNo lift

Each lift is short, about 40 to 45 minutes, and always hours after that day's run.

How the coaches settle a clash

Bo wants my legs strong. Kai wants them fresh for the hard days. When they disagree, Rook decides in the same order every time: pain I report first, then the marathon, then my weight, then strength. So the run usually wins, and Bo takes it better than you'd think.

Lower-body days land on the hard-run days, after the run, so my legs get a full day or more before the next hard effort. Saturday stays free of leg work before the Sunday long run.

Who this is for

Right now it coaches one runner: me. If it gets me to Chicago, the people on the waitlist get to try it first. It isn't medical advice, and the coaches are AI characters.
