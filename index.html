Why I'm doing this

I'll be 57 when she starts high school.

My son was born a few weeks after I turned 41. My daughter came this April. I’ll be 57 when she starts high school, and I want to still be out there playing with them.

I want them to see what working toward a goal actually looks like. They’ll see the training, the days I struggle, and whether I keep going when it gets hard. I hope that stays with them when they have something of their own they want to try.

And part of this is for me. I’ve run faster before, and I want to find out what I’m still capable of. I started this at 258 lb. Getting under three hours will take more than I’ve done before, but I want to give it a real try.

Where I've been

I've been faster.

In June 2024 I ran a half marathon in 1:32:31. That’s still my best. VDOT puts race distance and time into a single running fitness score. A higher score means a faster performance. It lets me compare a 10K with a half marathon and see how far I am from my marathon goal. Running under three hours would take me beyond my previous best.

VDOT is a performance estimate, not a lab measurement of oxygen use. The watch-based line is an estimate between races. Race-day conditions and marathon endurance still matter. More about VDOT.

Why I’m sharing it

I want you to see how it really goes.

I’m spending this year improving my AI coaches while they help me train. I want them to get better at helping me lose weight, perform and recover. My workouts and my feedback give me something to check their advice against.

There’s so much noise online that it can be hard to tell what someone actually did. I don’t want to make a good story out of training I didn’t do. My running stories start with my Garmin data, including the runs I wish had gone better.

I use animation to share those days and the conversations around them. The scenes are dramatized, but the training records are real. To me, AI is worth using if it helps me show who I am and connect with people.

I hope something here makes you want to work toward a goal of your own. And I’d love to build these coaches into something that can help you do that. For now, I’m finding out how well they can help me.
