Privacy, in plain words.

This is a one-person site. Here's what it collects and where that goes.

What the forms collect

The Sunday letter form asks for your email and an optional first name. The AI coaching waitlist form asks for your email and, optionally, which part of training is hardest to fit together. Please don’t send medical records through either form.

Where it goes

The forms send what you enter to Kit, the email service I use. The Sunday letter and the AI coaching waitlist are separate choices. Requesting one doesn’t sign you up for the other. I use the optional training answer to understand what people want from their own AI coaches.

What the site counts

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits, watch-link clicks and requests for the Sunday letter or places on the AI coaching waitlist. Those requests are counted separately; a form submission doesn’t tell me whether an email was delivered. It sets a cookie to tell one visit from the next. Your name, your email and anything you type are never sent to it. Episode 1 plays directly on this site. You can also open it on YouTube.

Getting off the list

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Or write to hello@tothefinish.ai and I'll take you off and delete what you sent.

The coaches

Rook, Kai, Bo, Juno and Mo are AI characters. This is my personal coaching setup, and nothing here is medical or nutrition advice.

Questions

Write to hello@tothefinish.ai.
