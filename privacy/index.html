Privacy, in plain words.

This is a one-person site. Here's what it collects and where that goes.

What the forms collect

The Sunday letter form asks for your first name and your email. The waitlist form asks for the same two, plus the race you're training for. That's all the forms ask for.

Where it goes

The forms send what you type to Kit, the email service I use to send the Sunday letter. I read the race you wrote so I know who I'm building this for.

What the site counts

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and sign-ups, and which race gets picked on the San Diego list. It sets a cookie to tell one visit from the next. Your name, your email and anything you type are never sent to it. Episode pages play the video through YouTube.

Getting off the list

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Or write to hello@tothefinish.ai and I'll take you off and delete what you sent.

The coaches

Rook, Kai, Bo, Juno and Mo are AI characters. This is my personal coaching setup, and nothing here is medical or nutrition advice.

Questions

Write to hello@tothefinish.ai.
